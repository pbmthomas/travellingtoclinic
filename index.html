Home to Eye Clinic Journey Planner

Plan a journey from your postcode to an NHS eye clinic or any postcode, or find the five eye units easiest for you to reach. Compare driving, cycling, walking and public transport.

Testing version. This is a prototype for testing only and is not an official NHS service. Journey times, routes, fares and clinic details come from third-party sources and may be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Always check with the transport operator and your appointment letter or clinic before you travel. The postcodes you search are sent to the mapping and transport services listed at the bottom of the page.

Tip: in the To box, just start typing an eye unit’s name, town or trust, or press ▾ to see them all.

Services used: postcodes.io, TfL Journey Planner, Transitous, OpenStreetMap routing (FOSSGIS / OSRM) and Google Fonts. Car fuel cost is an estimate from distance and your mpg; tolls, parking and congestion charges are not included. Public transport fares are TfL's where it returns one. Public transport comes from the TfL Journey Planner and from Transitous (open timetable data, © OpenStreetMap contributors and the data publishers listed there); road times assume typical traffic-free conditions.
